AI for Biology

Preserving context, structure, and evidence in biological prediction.

01 ＋
BioinformaticsShanghaiTech University · Course research

Context-specific synthetic lethality — interactions depend on context

Synthetic lethality is often treated as gene-pair classification, but the same pair can behave differently across cell lines. This project explored multimodal representations combining gene expression, scGPT, GenePT, ESM protein embeddings, and protein–protein interaction networks.

My work. Collected and processed data, helped design a GCN-based architecture, and benchmarked within-cell-line prediction alongside generalization to unseen cellular contexts. This work first made me treat biological context as part of the problem definition rather than a nuisance variable.

02 ✳
Computational oncologyShanghai Jiao Tong University

HappyRabbit — interpretable cancer drug response

HappyRabbit asks what a drug-response model should provide beyond low prediction error. The study built a common-panel benchmark of 122,178 drug–cell-line pairs, comparing ten models under identical splits and combining prediction with attribution analysis, in-silico perturbation, pathway analysis, and literature-based evidence auditing.

My work. Contributed to the PrototypeSCL / SparseProt model design, benchmark construction against nine baselines, and an attribution workflow using integrated gradients, virtual knockouts, and independent literature checks. PrototypeSCL achieved the strongest reported performance in this controlled comparison; the broader aim is to assess whether model explanations are biologically defensible.

03 ⌁
Computational neuroscienceShanghaiTech University

MouseGPT — describing behavior in natural language

MouseGPT reframes animal-behavior analysis as open-vocabulary multimodal understanding. It maps mouse videos into natural-language descriptions and semantic representations for behavior profiling, clustering, and discovery, using more than 42 million video frames.

My work. Reproduced the analysis pipeline and comparison methods, including pose-tracking baselines; evaluated model behavior on established benchmarks and improved the agent interface for downstream analysis. The project highlights how the chosen representation shapes what a model can discover.

Generative world models

Testing whether plausible generations reflect structured, causal dynamics.

04 ↳
Video generationShanghai Jiao Tong University

CausalCascade — evaluating how events unfold

CausalCascade evaluates whether video generation models can faithfully realize how events unfold through causal chains. It includes 50 simulator-grounded physical scenes, thousands of controlled variants, structured event graphs, and paired counterfactual interventions, and tests nine image-to-video models.

Research focus. Studying the gap between recovering what should happen and generating how it actually happens. The broader question is whether a visually convincing future reflects a model of the causal process that produced it.

Wearable sensingShanghaiTech University · PRP

Fabric wristband for human–machine interaction

A breathable fabric wristband with physiological and motion sensing, designed for comfortable, wireless monitoring and gesture recognition.

My work. Designed a Dynamic Time Warping approach to compare swing sequences and cluster forehand and backhand gestures despite variations in timing. This early project introduced me to the role representation plays in making sensor data useful.

A common thread

What are we asking the model to learn—and how would we know?

Biological context, semantic behavior, validated explanations, and causal event structure are different parts of the same challenge: define the capability a scientific task needs, then build evidence that tests it.
