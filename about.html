About me.

I’m Zhengning Xu, a third-year Computer Science and Technology undergraduate at ShanghaiTech University. I study how AI can help answer scientific questions in biology and the physical world.

我是许正宁，上海科技大学计算机科学与技术专业的大三本科生。

我关注人工智能如何帮助回答生物学与物理世界中的科学问题。

Research across disciplines.

I have conducted research at ShanghaiTech’s School of Information Science and Technology (SIST) and Shanghai Jiao Tong University’s School of Artificial Intelligence (SAI). My interests include computational biology, multimodal learning, and generative world models.

我曾在上海科技大学信息科学与技术学院（SIST）和上海交通大学人工智能学院（SAI）开展研究。

我的兴趣包括计算生物学、多模态学习和生成式世界模型。

Across these areas, I ask what a model must represent, what it should predict, and what evidence would make its answer scientifically useful.

在这些方向中，我始终关心：模型需要表征什么、应该预测什么，

以及怎样的证据才能让它的回答真正有助于科学研究。

Research philosophy / 研究理念

Choose the question before optimizing the answer.

AI makes it easier to build and improve models. The harder task is deciding which scientific question to ask and what would count as a meaningful answer.

人工智能让模型的构建与改进变得更容易。更难的是判断：

什么科学问题值得提出，什么样的结果才算有意义的回答。

I work from the scientific claim: define the capability a model should have, identify evidence that could test it, then choose the representation, model, or benchmark the question needs.

我会从科学主张反推研究设计：先明确模型应具备的能力，

找到能够检验它的证据，再决定需要什么表征、模型或基准。
