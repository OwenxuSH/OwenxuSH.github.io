About Me

I am Zhengning Xu, a third-year undergraduate majoring in Computer Science and Technology at ShanghaiTech University. I have conducted research at ShanghaiTech University’s School of Information Science and Technology (SIST) and at Shanghai Jiao Tong University’s School of Artificial Intelligence (SAI), where I am currently a research intern at DeepDelta Lab, under the supervision of Prof. Shaobo Cui.

My research interests lie in AI for Science, with work on biological data, drug discovery, animal behavior, and the evaluation of generative models for physical processes.

Research Interests

  • AI for Biology: genetic interactions, cancer drug response, and multimodal animal behavior analysis.
  • Physical-world modeling: wearable sensing and the causal evaluation of generative video models.
