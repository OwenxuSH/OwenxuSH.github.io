01 / About

Curiosity across disciplines.

I study Computer Science and Technology at ShanghaiTech University. My research interests center on AI for science, particularly computational biology, multimodal learning, and methods that make model outputs easier to test and interpret.

My work has taken me from synthetic lethality and cancer drug response to open-vocabulary animal behavior analysis. I have also worked on causal evaluation for video generation and motion analysis for wearable sensing. Across these settings, I am interested in the same question: what evidence tells us a model has learned something useful?

02 / Research

Selected projects

Questions, methods, and the work I contributed.

01 ✳
Computational oncologyShanghai Jiao Tong University

HappyRabbit — interpretable cancer drug response

A drug response system combining molecular graphs and cell-line gene expression with gene-level attribution, literature review, and evidence auditing.

My work. Contributed to the PrototypeSCL / SparseProt model design, a common-panel benchmark against nine baselines, and an attribution workflow using integrated gradients, virtual knockouts, and independent literature checks.

Drug responseGraph learningInterpretability
02 ⌁
Computational neuroscienceShanghaiTech University

MouseGPT — describing behavior in natural language

A vision-language framework that maps mouse videos to open-vocabulary behavioral descriptions, then uses those descriptions for profiling, clustering, and behavior discovery.

My work. Reproduced the analysis pipeline and comparison methods, including pose-tracking baselines; evaluated model behavior on established benchmarks and improved the agent interface for downstream analysis.

Vision-language modelsBehavior analysisBenchmarking
03 ↳
Video generationShanghai Jiao Tong University

CausalCascade — evaluating how events unfold

A simulator-grounded benchmark for whether generated videos preserve event chains and respond coherently to interventions. Its evaluations cover event understanding, motion localization, and counterfactual consistency.

Research focus. Studying evaluation that goes beyond visual plausibility to test whether model-generated dynamics follow the causal structure of a scene.

Video generationCausal reasoningEvaluation
04 ＋
BioinformaticsShanghaiTech University · Course research

Context-specific synthetic lethality prediction

A multi-omics framework for predicting genetic interactions within and across cancer cell lines, combining cell-line-specific protein interaction graphs with gene and protein representations.

My work. Collected and processed data, helped design the GCN-based architecture, and benchmarked single-cell-line and cross-cell-line prediction settings.

Multi-omicsGCNSynthetic lethality
05 〰
Wearable sensingShanghaiTech University · PRP

Fabric wristband for human–machine interaction

A breathable fabric wristband with physiological and motion sensing, designed for comfortable, wireless monitoring and gesture recognition.

My work. Designed a Dynamic Time Warping approach to compare swing sequences and cluster forehand and backhand gestures despite variations in timing.

Time-series analysisDTWHuman–machine interaction
