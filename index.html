01 / AI for Biology

From genetic interactions to animal behavior.

I have studied context-specific synthetic lethality, helped build a large common-panel drug-response benchmark, and evaluated MouseGPT's open-vocabulary analysis of animal behavior.

These projects have given me experience with biological data integration, model design, rigorous comparison, and multimodal evaluation.

02 / Generative world models

Evaluating how generated worlds evolve.

CausalCascade uses 50 simulator-grounded physical scenes, structured event graphs, and paired interventions to evaluate nine image-to-video models.

The project asks whether models can realize complete chains of events, a step toward world models whose behavior can be tested under change.

The question behind my work

What should AI optimize for?

Across these projects, I keep returning to one question: what must an AI system capture before its output becomes scientifically meaningful? In biology, that may be cellular context or a representation of behavior that supports discovery. In video generation, it may be the causal structure of a sequence of events.

As AI becomes better at optimizing the objectives we give it, I want to help choose objectives and evaluations that address the scientific bottleneck. This question connects my research experiences; the work itself spans several fields of AI for Science.
