The question behind my work

What must a model capture before its output becomes scientifically meaningful?

As coding agents make well-defined objectives easier to optimize, I find myself looking further upstream: at the question, representation, and evidence that define success.

A lower error, a plausible video, or a fluent explanation can each conceal a mismatch between what a model produces and what science needs to know. My work examines those gaps across biology and generative models.
